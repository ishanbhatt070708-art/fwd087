How it works

  1. Register with your name, college and student ID.
  2. Browse trips or search by destination, date and budget.
  3. Join a trip, or create your own and set the number of seats.
  4. See who is travelling with you and pack your bags.

Why students choose TripMate

  • ID-based registration builds a trusted student community.
  • One central place instead of scattered WhatsApp groups.
  • Clear cost, dates and seats before you decide.
  • Works on mobile and desktop.
