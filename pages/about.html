About TripMate

TripMate is a student travel companion platform where university students discover trips, find companions and plan journeys together.

Find your group. Plan your trip. Travel together.

Browse trips Contact us

The problem we want to solve

Finding companions
Friends have clashing plans, and travelling with strangers feels unsafe.
Managing trip costs
Per-person costs are hard to estimate and split.
Planning the trip
Details are scattered across chats and social media.

Students need one trusted place to find, join and plan trips together.

How TripMate works

  1. Discover: search and filter student trips by destination, budget and date.
  2. Compare: see the destination, estimated cost per student and seats left.
  3. Join or host: request a seat on a trip, or create your own trip.
  4. Travel together: go with fellow students.

Built around trust

  • A student-only community.
  • Student ID required at registration.
  • Clear host and trip details before you join.
  • Ratings and reports (planned).
