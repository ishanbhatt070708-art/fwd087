Quick answers

Who can join a trip?

Only registered students. You register with your name, college and student ID.

How do I join a trip?

Login, open the Trips page, check the cost and seats, then request to join.

How do I host a trip?

Login and open Host a Trip. Pick a destination, set the cost and seats, then publish.

Are the costs exact?

No. Each trip shows an estimated cost per person so you can compare before you join.
